Getting started

What do I need to use Novelwave?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, or an iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. To use it on your wrist, you’ll need an Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or later. To sync your library between devices, sign in to iCloud on each of them.

Can I play audiobooks from Audible or Apple Books?

No. Books bought there are protected with DRM and only play in their own apps. Novelwave plays audiobook files you can keep: DRM‑free downloads, CDs you’ve ripped, and public‑domain recordings.

Where can I get DRM‑free audiobooks?

Stores like Libro.fm and Downpour sell many audiobooks as DRM‑free downloads, usually as MP3 or M4B files. LibriVox offers thousands of public‑domain classics read by volunteers. You can also rip audiobook CDs to MP3 or M4B.

How do I add audiobooks?

In Library, tap + and choose Local Files or iCloud Drive, then pick as many audio files as you like. The M4B guide walks through it step by step, from getting the file onto your iPhone.

When you save, choose Save Separately to make each file its own audiobook, or Merge Into One Audiobook to combine them, with your own title, author and cover. Books land on the shelf you’re browsing, or you can choose another. The MP3 guide shows how to turn a folder of MP3 files into one book.

You can also tap an audiobook in the Files app, or share one or several from another app, and they open in Novelwave.

Which file formats are supported?

M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC, Ogg Opus, FLAC, WAV, AIFF and MP4. Opus plays natively, without re-encoding, and an MP4 that holds video comes in as just its audio. If a file has chapters, Novelwave shows them in the player.

How do I import from iCloud Drive?

Go to Profile, then Sources, and connect iCloud Drive. Novelwave creates its own folder in your iCloud Drive. Put your audiobooks in that folder using the Files app, then import them from iCloud Drive in Library.

Library and sync

How do I organize my library?

Create shelves with the shelf button in Library, and put shelves inside other shelves as deep as you like. Swipe on an audiobook to move it, mark it as finished, reset its progress and more.

To sort, filter or move several audiobooks at once, use the ⋯ menu.

How does sync work?

Your shelves, audiobook details, chapters, bookmarks and listening progress sync through iCloud to every device signed in to the same Apple Account. Each device downloads its own copy of an audiobook’s audio when you play it.

Why does an audiobook say it isn’t on this device?

Its audio is stored in iCloud and hasn’t been downloaded to this device yet. Tap it to download it. In the meantime, you can still edit and organize it.

Why are some details missing from a book I imported from iCloud Drive?

An audiobook’s full details become available after its first download finishes. They stay available even if you remove the download later.

Playback

How do I change the playback speed?

In the player, tap the speed button at the bottom left. Choose anywhere from 0.5× to 6× for the current audiobook.

How do I set a sleep timer?

In the player, tap the moon button. Choose a number of minutes, or stop at the end of the current chapter or file.

How do bookmarks work?

Tap the bookmark button in the player to add a bookmark at your current position. You can find, rename and add notes to your bookmarks in the player’s chapters and bookmarks list.

Can I change how far rewind and fast-forward skip?

Yes. In Profile, open Settings to choose skip durations from 5 to 45 seconds. You’ll also find options to rewind a little whenever you pause, resume after interruptions, and start playing as soon as you open an audiobook.

Can I control playback from my headphones?

Yes. On compatible headphones, press once to play or pause, twice to skip forward and three times to skip back. You can also control playback from the Lock Screen and Control Center.

How do I add the Now Playing widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Novelwave. The widget comes in small and medium sizes.

A widget shows whatever you’re playing. To keep one on a particular audiobook, touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose the audiobook.

Apple Watch, CarPlay and Siri

How do I use Novelwave on Apple Watch?

Install Novelwave on your watch from the App Store on Apple Watch, or from the Watch app on your iPhone. Your library arrives through iCloud, so sign in to the same Apple Account on both.

While your iPhone is playing, the watch’s player controls it. To listen with the iPhone left at home, download the book to the watch and connect Bluetooth headphones.

How do I put an audiobook on my watch?

On the watch, swipe left on an audiobook to download it. On your iPhone, swipe on an audiobook in Library and choose Send to Apple Watch. You can pause or resume a transfer, and remove a book from the watch when you’re done with it. The Apple Watch guide walks through it step by step.

How do I use Novelwave in CarPlay?

Connect your iPhone to CarPlay and open Novelwave. Continue shows the books you’re listening to, with the time left on each, Library lets you browse your shelves, and Now Playing keeps skip, speed and chapters in reach.

What can I ask Siri?

Ask Siri to resume your audiobook in Novelwave, skip ahead or back, go to the next chapter, change the speed, set the sleep timer or add a bookmark. With Apple Intelligence on iOS 27, you can ask for a book by name.

Every one of these is also an action in the Shortcuts app, for your own automations.

Listening Stats

What do Listening Stats show?

The Stats tab shows how long you’ve listened by week, month and year, the books you finished and the ones in progress, and the speeds you listened at. Listening on Apple Watch counts too, and your history syncs through iCloud.

How do I fix a book in my stats?

In Stats, touch and hold a book to change the date you finished it, mark it as not finished, or hide it from your stats. You can also swipe on a finished book to hide it.

What happens to my stats if I delete an audiobook?

Your listening stays in your stats, with the book’s title and author, so deleting a book never takes away the hours you spent with it.

Storage and data

How do I free up space?

Swipe on an audiobook imported from iCloud Drive and remove its download. It stays in your library and can be downloaded again at any time.

You can also use Recover storage in Profile to clear away files Novelwave no longer uses. It doesn’t affect your audiobooks.

How do I back up my library?

In Profile, choose Export your library to save a complete copy to any folder in Files. To export a single audiobook, swipe on it and choose Export to Files.

How do I start over?

In Profile, choose Reset your library. This permanently removes all shelves, audiobooks, bookmarks and progress from all your devices, and it can’t be undone, so export a copy first if you might need it.
