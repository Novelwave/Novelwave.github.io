Audiobook player for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

For the audiobooks you already own.

Play your DRM‑free M4B, MP3 and other audiobook files with their chapters, and pick up where you left off on every device.

★ 4.8 on the App Store

How it works Bring your own audiobooks.

  1. Get your files

    DRM‑free audiobooks from stores like Libro.fm and Downpour, CDs you’ve ripped, or public‑domain classics from LibriVox.

  2. Import them

    From Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or a computer. A book in several parts becomes one.

  3. Listen anywhere

    Your place, shelves and bookmarks follow you to iPad, Apple Watch and the car through your own iCloud. No account, no server.

Plays M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Opus and MP4. Audible’s AAX files only play in Audible’s app.

Player Made for long books.

  • Chapters

    Skip by chapter and see how far into one you are. The full list is a tap away.

  • Speed

    0.5× to 6× in steps of 0.05, with the time left at each speed.

  • Sleep timer

    Five minutes to the end of the chapter, counting down on the Lock Screen.

  • Bookmarks

    Save a moment in one tap, and a note to say why.

Plus skips from 5 to 45 seconds, a short rewind when you pause, and playback that picks up again after calls and alarms.

Library Shelves, like a real bookcase.

Shelves inside shelves. Sanderson, then Cosmere, then Mistborn. Nest them as deep as your collection goes.

Find any book. Search titles, authors and shelves, or show only what you’re in the middle of.

Apple Watch Leave the phone at home.

Send a book to your Apple Watch before a run, or keep your iPhone in your pocket and steer it from your wrist. How it works on Apple Watch

CarPlay and Siri Hands on the wheel.

Carry on where you left off, or browse your shelves. Then just ask.

  • “Skip back 30 seconds.”
  • “Next chapter.”
  • “Stop at the end of this chapter.”

And more.

  • Shortcuts

    Actions for playback, chapters, speed, bookmarks and the sleep timer.

  • Lock Screen

    The sleep timer counts down there and in the Dynamic Island.

  • Widgets

    Now Playing on your Home Screen, and controls in Control Center.

  • Listening stats

    By week, month and year, with a look back every January.

  • Every detail

    Titles, authors and covers from Photos or Files, yours to change.

  • Export

    Your whole library to Files, bookmarks and notes included.

  • Spotlight

    Find a book from your Home Screen’s search.

  • Eight languages

    With a language setting of its own.

Privacy No account. No ads. No tracking.

No subscription to play the books you own. Your library lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, and shelves you hide stay behind Face ID.

On the App Store, Novelwave’s privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”. Read the privacy policy
