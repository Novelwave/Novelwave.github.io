Roadmap

Where Novelwave is headed

Choose a stop on the map to see what’s in it. There are no dates, and plans can change as I learn what listeners need most.

Jump to 3.3, out now · 3.4, coming soon · Later

Last updated November 26, 2026

  1. A new chapter: iCloud sync, iCloud Drive import, export, a livelier Player, Now Playing widgets and a cat for company.

    What’s in Novelwave 3.0

    A new chapter

    • Refreshed design. A refreshed library, player and profile make browsing, listening and managing your audiobooks easier.
    • Sync between devices. Shelves, audiobook details, listening progress and bookmarks stay in sync through iCloud.
    • Import from iCloud Drive. Add audiobooks to Novelwave’s iCloud Drive folder, then download, pause, resume or remove them whenever you like.
    • Export your audiobooks. Save single audiobooks, a selection, shelves or your whole library to Files.
    • Your 1.x library, brought across. On iPhones that stayed on Novelwave 1.x, your audiobooks, shelves and bookmarks move into the new app, and your old library is never deleted.
    • Become a beta tester. Request a TestFlight invitation from Profile to try new features early.

    A Player with more presence

    • A cover that comes alive. It sits forward while playing and eases back when paused, over a background tinted with its color.
    • A scrubber you can grab. A slim track with a knob that never jumps playback when you touch it.
    • A new speed control. A smooth slider with firm stops at the presets, showing how long the rest of the book takes at that speed.
    • Bookmarks in one tap. Save one straight away, then add a note, and find chapters and bookmarks together in one Contents sheet.
    • A finish worth celebrating. Confetti when you reach the end of an audiobook.

    Around the app

    • Now Playing widgets. Small and medium, with the cover, play and pause, and skip buttons on the medium size.
    • Choose your app icon. Keep the book, or switch to Nova, the cat in headphones, in Settings.
    • A cat for company. Empty shelves, searches, bookmarks, chapters and widgets have a resident cat, who waves back when you tap it in Profile.
    • Text at your size. Every screen follows your Dynamic Type setting.
    • Smooth with big libraries. Covers load once at the size they’re shown, and imports show how far along they are.

  2. CarPlay, Siri and Shortcuts, Control Center, Opus and MP4 support, a Minimal style, and a quicker library.

    What’s new in Novelwave 3.1

    In the car and hands-free

    • CarPlay. Pick up where you left off with cover cards and the time left on each book, or browse your shelves, with chapters a tap away.
    • Siri. Play or resume an audiobook, skip ahead or back, jump to a chapter, change the speed, set the sleep timer or add a bookmark, just by asking.
    • Ask for a book by name. With Apple Intelligence on iOS 27, say “play The Martian” without naming the app.
    • Shortcuts. Actions to play, pause, skip, change chapter, set the speed and add bookmarks, for your own automations.

    On your iPhone

    • A sleep timer Live Activity. Watch the sleep timer count down on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and cancel it right there.
    • Control Center. Play and pause, or start and stop a sleep timer that shows when playback will stop, without opening the app.
    • Spotlight and Siri Suggestions. Find any audiobook from Spotlight, and see what you’ve been listening to suggested on the Lock Screen.

    Bringing books in

    • Open with Novelwave. Tap an M4B, M4A, MP3 or Opus file in Files, or share one from another app, and it opens in Novelwave.
    • Several files at once. Select a handful of audio files and share them to Novelwave in one go.
    • Opus audiobooks. Ogg Opus files import and play natively, without re-encoding.
    • MP4 files too. Audiobooks that Google Drive exports as .mp4 import like any other, and a file that holds video comes in as just its audio.

    Make it yours

    • Illustrated or Minimal. Keep the cat, or choose a Minimal style without illustrations.
    • The original icon is back. Novelwave’s first icon, headphones over a book of dunes, joins the picker as Classic.
    • Keep listening while you bookmark. Turn off “Pause when adding a bookmark” in Settings, and playback carries on as you save one.

    Improvements

    • A quicker library. Oversized covers are shrunk once in the background, and the Library refreshes once for a burst of changes, so big libraries scroll smoothly.

    Fixes

    • No waiting on iCloud. Siri, widgets and CarPlay open Novelwave straight away.
    • No empty flashes. Screens no longer show their empty state for a moment before their content appears.
    • The right ending. The last chapter of some files no longer runs past the end of the book.

  3. Novelwave on Apple Watch, listening stats and your Year in Listening, a widget for every book, bookmarks you can share, and your latest place on every device.

    What’s new in Novelwave 3.2

    On your wrist

    • Apple Watch. Browse your library, play what’s on your iPhone, or download audiobooks to the watch and leave the phone at home.
    • Everything in reach. Speed, sleep timer, chapters and bookmarks on either device, with notes you can dictate.
    • A Player like Now Playing. The whole cover over a wash of its colors, with the part playing and the time left in it, and play ringed by its progress.
    • Pick up on either device. Carry on listening on the iPhone or the watch, right where you were.
    • On the watch face. Complications that curve the part playing over its progress or show Nova, and a Smart Stack widget.
    • Downloads you can manage. Pause and resume a transfer, see the watch’s storage on your iPhone, and remove a book from the watch when you’re done.

    Your listening

    • Listening stats. How long you’ve listened by week, month and year, the books you finished and the ones in progress, and the speeds you listened at.
    • Your Year in Listening. A look back at your year, with a reminder on January 1.
    • Share your stats. Turn any period into a story-sized card that catches the light as you tilt your iPhone, then share it or save it to Photos.
    • Stats that tell the truth. Hide a book from your stats, mark one as not finished, or change the date you finished it, right from a swipe.
    • Deleted books still count. Delete an audiobook and its listening stays in your stats on every device, where you can still edit or hide it.

    Around the app

    • A widget for every book. Set each Now Playing widget to an audiobook of its own, or leave it following whatever you’re playing.
    • Share your bookmarks. Send an audiobook’s bookmarks and notes from the bookmark list, and find them in every Files export, grouped by chapter.
    • Long-press for more. Shelves, audiobooks, bookmarks and files waiting to import open a menu with the same actions as their swipes.

    Across your devices

    • Your latest place, every time. Open a book or come back to the app and Novelwave checks iCloud first, so you pick up where you stopped on your other device.
    • Sync that keeps going. Sync now starts on every launch and recovers by itself in the rare cases where it used to stop.

    Bringing books in

    • Straight to the shelf you’re on. Add audiobooks while browsing a shelf and they land there, and choosing another shelf starts from where you are.
    • Quicker imports. Novelwave reads several files at once.
    • Titles from the tags. Merge several files into one audiobook and its title and author are filled in from their tags.

    Improvements

    • Faster all round. Novelwave opens straight to your library, and the Player, Mini Player, search and sync all do less work as you listen.
    • Smoother screens. Empty screens fade into their content instead of switching abruptly.

    Fixes

    • Ready for iOS 27.2. Novelwave no longer crashes right after opening with iCloud turned on, on early iOS 27.2 betas.
    • No crash on a damaged library. Novelwave sets it aside and syncs your library back in, instead of crashing on launch.
    • Room to open. If your iPhone is too full to open your library, Novelwave asks you to free up space instead of crashing, and your library stays where it is.
    • Fewer crashes. No more crashes in the Library when an audiobook has a broken length, or in the background after restarting your iPhone.
    • Your place, kept. Your place in a book is saved as soon as you leave the app.
    • Your old library, brought across. Bringing over a library from the previous version of Novelwave no longer gets stuck. If it can’t finish, try again, or continue and pick it up later.
    • Other apps keep playing. Opening Novelwave no longer stops their audio until you press play.
    • Steady CarPlay covers. Covers no longer flicker when a list refreshes.
    • Exact lengths and skips. Variable-bitrate MP3s and long Ogg audiobooks show their true length, and skips and reopened books land exactly where they should.
    • Widgets on a clear Home Screen. The Now Playing widget no longer turns into a white block on a tinted or clear Home Screen.
    • Search keeps up. Results always match what you typed last.
    • Covers stay crisp. Editing a book’s details no longer re-saves its cover.
    • Finished stays finished. Listening to a book again keeps its finish in your stats.
    • Keeps playing in the background. Audiobooks with chapter images no longer pause when you leave Novelwave or lock your iPhone.
    • No false finishes. A file that stops short of its end keeps your place instead of marking the audiobook finished.
    • Chapters open where you are. The chapter list opens on the chapter you’re playing, and the whole row takes a tap.

  4. Novelwave on iPad, simpler imports, hidden shelves, a new Player on Apple Watch, a true‑black Midnight theme, and seven more languages.

    What’s new in Novelwave 3.3

    On iPad

    • iPad. A layout made for the bigger screen, with your tabs and shelves in a sidebar, and shelves and search results as grids of covers.
    • Drag and drop. Drag covers to reorder or move them, and drag a book out to another app.
    • More than one window. Open Novelwave side by side, with settings and imports kept in step across windows.
    • Keyboard and pointer. Menu bar commands with shortcuts like ⌘O to import and ⌘, for Settings, and controls that light up under the pointer.
    • A Player with room to breathe. Chapters and bookmarks in a column beside the Player when the window is wide enough, and speed in a popover.

    Bringing books in

    • Importing, simplified. Everything happens on one screen: choose whether your files become one book or several, and check each book’s title, author and cover before saving. New books are highlighted in your library once they’re in.
    • A new iCloud Drive browser. Pick files and folders together, search every folder at once, and sort the list. Each folder you pick becomes its own book.
    • Drop files in. Drop audio files or whole folders onto your library on iPhone or iPad to import them.
    • Whole folders from Files. Choose folders in the import menu, not just files.

    On your wrist

    • A new Player on Apple Watch. Laid out like Now Playing, over your cover’s own colors. Turn the volume with the Digital Crown, swipe left for the sleep timer, speed, chapters and bookmarks, and see the chapter playing in a complication.

    While you listen

    • Autoplay next book. When a book ends, the next one on its shelf or in Library starts playing. Turn it on in Settings.
    • Stats that outlast a book. Delete a book and its listening time stays in Stats, where you can still edit its details.

    Kept private

    • Hidden shelves. Hide a shelf behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Its books stay out of Search, Stats, widgets, CarPlay and Apple Watch, and show as Private Audiobook on the Lock Screen.

    Make it yours

    • Midnight. A true‑black theme beside Light and Dark, which OLED screens draw with their pixels off.
    • Grid or list. Show your shelves as a grid of covers or as a list, on iPhone too. Pick one from a shelf’s More menu.

    In your language

    • Seven more languages. Use Novelwave on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch in German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Romanian or Russian, and pick its language in Settings. What’s New is translated too.

    Improvements

    • Better with VoiceOver and larger text. Clearer labels, layouts that adapt at the largest text sizes, bigger tap targets, and stronger colors when Increase Contrast is on.
    • Edits show up straight away. Change a book’s details and the open Player follows along.

  5. Audiobookshelf, ratings and reviews, details found online, Lock Screen widgets, and a library of just the books on this device.

    Want 3.4 before it ships? Install Novelwave, then ask to join the beta from Profile. Get Novelwave

    Coming in Novelwave 3.4

    Your own server

    • Audiobookshelf. Connect your server in Sources, then browse its libraries, series, authors, narrators and collections. Books you add stream straight away, and your place stays in step with the server.
    • Series as shelves. Add a series from your server and it lands on a shelf named after it.
    • In the car. Server audiobooks play in CarPlay, and grey out while the server can’t be reached.
    • Clear when the server is slow. The play button shows when a stream is buffering, and the Player says when it’s waiting for your server.

    Rate and review

    • Stars and a review. Rate a book from its menu, or when you finish it if you’d like to be asked.
    • Ratings on your covers. Stars show on your books in Library and in Stats.
    • Take your notes with you. Export your bookmarks and reviews as text, and your ratings as a spreadsheet, from Profile.

    Book details

    • Find details online. Search Open Library from Edit Audiobook, then choose field by field between your details and what it found.
    • Fix My Library. Check your whole library for missing covers, authors, years, genres and descriptions, then apply them all at once or one book at a time.
    • Details for new imports. Turn it on, and books you import get their missing details looked up straight away. Only titles and authors are sent to Open Library.

    Your library

    • Just the books on this device. One setting keeps audiobooks whose audio isn’t on this device out of your library. Search still finds them.
    • Downloads that fit. Novelwave tells you how much space a download needs before starting one that won’t fit.
    • Downloads that stay. Disconnect iCloud Drive and the books that are fully downloaded stay in your library.

    On your Lock Screen

    • Lock Screen widgets. Play or pause from a ring of your progress, or see the chapter and when the book ends beside the clock.

    Improvements

    • Easier to tap. The Player’s option buttons are full-size targets on every iPhone and iPad.
    • Whole covers. Small covers fit their square instead of being cropped.

  6. Novelwave on iPhone Duo, the Mac and Apple TV, EPUB books to read and listen to, books on every device through iCloud Drive, more places for your books, a sound boost, more widgets, and levels to earn.

    Further down the road

    More screens

    • iPhone Duo. A layout that makes the most of the big inner screen, and carries on from the outer one when you fold it shut.
    • Mac. Listen at your desk and manage a large library with a keyboard and pointer.
    • Apple TV. Play your book through the living room speakers and pick up right where you left off on your iPhone.

    Read and listen

    • EPUB books. Add the ebook alongside its audiobook, and read or listen to the same story in one app.

    Books on every device

    • Upload to iCloud Drive. Send books you added from files on one device to iCloud Drive, and they play on all your devices, with their progress, bookmarks and shelves just as they were.
    • Find your audiobook files. Reinstalled Novelwave, or moved to a new phone without a backup? Choose the folders that hold your files, and Novelwave matches them to your books, progress included.

    More places for your books

    • Dropbox. Bring in audiobooks from your Dropbox.
    • Google Drive. Bring in audiobooks from your Google Drive.
    • Jellyfin. Connect Novelwave to your Jellyfin server.
    • Public‑domain classics. Browse and download public‑domain audiobooks from catalogs like LibriVox, right inside Novelwave.

    While you listen

    • Sound boost. Make quiet recordings louder, so a soft‑spoken narrator is easy to hear wherever you are.

    On your Home Screen

    • More widgets. New kinds of widgets, beyond Now Playing.

    Your listening

    • Levels and experience. Earn experience as you listen and level up along the way.
