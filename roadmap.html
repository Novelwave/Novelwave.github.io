Roadmap

Where Novelwave is headed

Choose a stop on the map to see what's in it. There are no dates, and plans can change as we learn what listeners need most.

Last updated October 27, 2026

  1. A new chapter: iCloud sync, iCloud Drive import, export and a refreshed design.

    What's in Novelwave 3.0

    A new chapter

    • Refreshed design. A refreshed library, player and profile make browsing, listening and managing your audiobooks easier.
    • Sync between devices. Shelves, audiobook details, listening progress and bookmarks stay in sync through iCloud.
    • Import from iCloud Drive. Add audiobooks to Novelwave's iCloud Drive folder, then download, pause, resume or remove them whenever you like.
    • Export your audiobooks. Save single audiobooks, a selection, shelves or your whole library to Files.
    • Become a beta tester. Request a TestFlight invitation from Profile to try new features early.

  2. Now Playing widgets, steadier scrubbing and a quicker Mini Player.

    What's new in Novelwave 3.0.1

    New

    • Now Playing widgets. Small and medium, with the cover, play and pause, skip buttons on the medium size, and a tap that opens the book.

    Fixes

    • Steady scrubbing. The progress bar no longer jumps back while you drag it during playback.
    • Finished books start over. Playing an audiobook marked as finished starts it from the beginning, instead of ending straight away and closing the Player.

    Improvements

    • An instant Mini Player cover. The cover appears straight away, without a placeholder flashing first.
    • Smoother while listening. The tab bar and Mini Player no longer redraw every second during playback.

  3. A Player with more presence, a cat for company, and an app icon of your choice.

    What's new in Novelwave 3.0.2

    A Player with more presence

    • A cover that comes alive. It sits forward while playing and eases back when paused, over a background tinted with its color.
    • A scrubber you can grab. A slim track with a knob that never jumps playback when you touch it.
    • A new speed control. A smooth slider with firm stops at the presets, showing how long the rest of the book takes at that speed.
    • Controls that respond. Animated symbols and haptic feedback.
    • Progress in the Mini Player. A thin line shows how far through the book you are.
    • A finish worth celebrating. Confetti when you reach the end of an audiobook.
    • The right cover for every file. Merged audiobooks show each file's own cover again.

    Around the app

    • Choose your app icon. Keep the book, or switch to Nova, the cat in headphones, in Settings.
    • A cat for company. Empty shelves, searches, bookmarks, chapters and widgets now have a resident cat, who waves back when you tap it in Profile.
    • Import from an empty shelf. An empty shelf offers to import audiobooks straight away.
    • Import progress. Reading a batch of files shows how far along it is.
    • Move a shelf with a swipe. Shelves can be moved from their swipe menu, like audiobooks.
    • Text at your size. Every screen follows your Dynamic Type setting.
    • Snappier covers. Covers load once and are decoded at the size they're shown, so lists scroll smoothly.

    Fixes

    • Everything in its place. New audiobooks and shelves land at the end of a shelf, and reordering no longer crashes.

  4. Bookmarks in one tap, and chapters and bookmarks in one place.

    What's new in Novelwave 3.0.3

    Improvements

    • Bookmarks in one tap. Tap the bookmark button to save it straight away, then add a note or jump to your bookmarks.
    • Chapters and bookmarks together. One Contents sheet, with a switch between chapters and bookmarks.

    Fixes

    • No flash on launch. Your Library no longer shows its empty state for a moment before your books appear.

  5. Your Novelwave 1.x library comes across, plus a steadier Player.

    What's new in Novelwave 3.0.4

    New

    • Your 1.x library, brought across. On iPhones that stayed on Novelwave 1.x, your audiobooks, shelves and bookmarks now move into the new app instead of it opening empty.
    • Progress you can see. A big library shows how many audiobooks have moved in, and the screen stays awake until it's done.
    • Nothing left behind. The move never deletes your old library.

    Fixes

    • The right button from the start. The Player no longer shows Play for a moment when your audiobook is already playing.
    • A steady sleep timer menu. The menu no longer flickers while it's open.
    • No more waiting on setup. Your library appears without waiting on iCloud, so launch can't get stuck on “Setting up your library…”.

  6. Fixes for libraries moving across from Novelwave 1.x.

    What's new in Novelwave 3.0.5

    Fixes

    • No crash while setting up. Large libraries no longer crash on “Setting up your library…” when updating from an older version, mostly seen on iPhones running iOS 17.
    • Every shelf comes across. An older library now moves in even when two of its shelves were saved with the same ID.
    • Your books are still there. If your library looked empty after a recent update, open Novelwave 3.0.5 and your audiobooks, shelves and bookmarks will be brought across.

  7. CarPlay, Siri and Shortcuts, Control Center, Opus and MP4 support, a Minimal style, and a quicker library.

    What's new in Novelwave 3.1

    In the car and hands-free

    • CarPlay. Pick up where you left off with cover cards and the time left on each book, or browse your shelves, with chapters a tap away.
    • Siri. Play or resume an audiobook, skip ahead or back, jump to a chapter, change the speed, set the sleep timer or add a bookmark, just by asking.
    • Ask for a book by name. With Apple Intelligence on iOS 27, say “play The Martian” without naming the app.
    • Shortcuts. Actions to play, pause, skip, change chapter, set the speed and add bookmarks, for your own automations.

    On your iPhone

    • A sleep timer Live Activity. Watch the sleep timer count down on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and cancel it right there.
    • Control Center. Play and pause, or start and stop a sleep timer that shows when playback will stop, without opening the app.
    • Spotlight and Siri Suggestions. Find any audiobook from Spotlight, and see what you've been listening to suggested on the Lock Screen.

    Bringing books in

    • Open with Novelwave. Tap an M4B, M4A, MP3 or Opus file in Files, or share one from another app, and it opens in Novelwave.
    • Several files at once. Select a handful of audio files and share them to Novelwave in one go.
    • Opus audiobooks. Ogg Opus files import and play natively, without re-encoding.
    • MP4 files too. Audiobooks that Google Drive exports as .mp4 import like any other, and a file that holds video comes in as just its audio.

    Make it yours

    • Illustrated or Minimal. Keep the cat, or choose a Minimal style without illustrations.
    • The original icon is back. Novelwave's first icon, headphones over a book of dunes, joins the picker as Classic.
    • Keep listening while you bookmark. Turn off “Pause when adding a bookmark” in Settings, and playback carries on as you save one.

    Improvements

    • A quicker library. Oversized covers are shrunk once in the background, and the Library refreshes once for a burst of changes, so big libraries scroll smoothly.

    Fixes

    • No waiting on iCloud. Siri, widgets and CarPlay open Novelwave straight away.
    • No empty flashes. Screens no longer show their empty state for a moment before their content appears.
    • The right ending. The last chapter of some files no longer runs past the end of the book.

  8. A tidier Mini Player and Player on iOS 17 and 18.

    Coming in Novelwave 3.1.1

    Fixes

    • The Mini Player in its place. On iOS 17 and 18 it sits above the tab bar as a full-width bar, and stays behind the keyboard while you type.
    • Room for the Player's options. On iPhones without a Home indicator, the options row no longer sits on the bottom edge.

  9. Novelwave on Apple Watch, listening stats and your Year in Listening, a widget for every book, bookmarks you can share, and your latest place on every device.

    Coming in Novelwave 3.2

    On your wrist

    • Apple Watch. Browse your library, play what's on your iPhone, or download audiobooks to the watch and leave the phone at home.
    • Everything in reach. Speed, sleep timer, chapters and bookmarks on either device, with notes you can dictate and volume on the Digital Crown.
    • Pick up on either device. Carry on listening on the iPhone or the watch, right where you were.
    • On the watch face. A Now Playing complication and a Smart Stack widget.
    • Downloads you can manage. Pause and resume a transfer, see the watch's storage on your iPhone, and remove a book from the watch when you're done.

    Your listening

    • Listening stats. How long you've listened by week, month and year, the books you finished and the ones in progress, and the speeds you listened at.
    • Your Year in Listening. A look back at your year, with a reminder on January 1.
    • Share your stats. Turn any period into a story-sized card that catches the light as you tilt your iPhone, then share it or save it to Photos.
    • Stats that tell the truth. Hide a book from your stats, mark one as not finished, or change the date you finished it.

    Around the app

    • A widget for every book. Set each Now Playing widget to an audiobook of its own, or leave it following whatever you're playing.
    • Share your bookmarks. Send an audiobook's bookmarks and notes from the bookmark list, and find them in every Files export, grouped by chapter.
    • Long-press for more. Shelves, audiobooks, bookmarks and files waiting to import open a menu with the same actions as their swipes.

    Across your devices

    • Your latest place, every time. Open a book or come back to the app and Novelwave checks iCloud first, so you pick up where you stopped on your other device.
    • Sync that keeps going. Sync now starts on every launch and recovers by itself in the rare cases where it used to stop.

    Bringing books in

    • Straight to the shelf you're on. Add audiobooks while browsing a shelf and they land there, and choosing another shelf starts from where you are.
    • Quicker imports. Novelwave reads several files at once.

    Improvements

    • Faster all round. Novelwave opens straight to your library, and the Player, Mini Player, search and sync all do less work as you listen.
    • Smoother screens. Empty screens fade into their content instead of switching abruptly.

    Fixes

    • No crash on a damaged library. Novelwave sets it aside and syncs your library back in, instead of crashing on launch.
    • Other apps keep playing. Opening Novelwave no longer stops their audio until you press play.
    • Steady CarPlay covers. Covers no longer flicker when a list refreshes.
    • Search keeps up. Results always match what you typed last.
    • Covers stay crisp. Editing a book's details no longer re-saves its cover.
    • Finished stays finished. Listening to a book again keeps its finish in your stats.

  10. Novelwave in seven more languages, and a few things made quicker.

    Planned for Novelwave 3.3

    In your language

    • Seven more languages. Use Novelwave in German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Romanian or Russian.

    Improvements

    • Tap to download. Tap a book that isn't on your iPhone and it starts downloading, with progress in its row.
    • Bigger Player buttons. The Player's options are easier to hit.

    Fixes

    • The right screen, every time. Tapping a notification, a Spotlight result or a widget opens what you tapped, even when Novelwave wasn't running.

  11. Novelwave on iPad, your Audiobookshelf server, and Lock Screen widgets.

    Planned for Novelwave 3.4

    Beyond iPhone

    • iPad. A layout made for the bigger screen.

    More places for your books

    • Audiobookshelf. Connect your server, stream your books, and keep your progress in sync with it.
    • Move to iCloud Drive. Move books you imported from Files into iCloud Drive, so they reach your other devices.

    On your Lock Screen

    • Lock Screen widgets. See and control what you're listening to, right from the Lock Screen.

  12. Novelwave on iPhone Duo and the Mac, EPUB read-along, more places for your books, more widgets, and levels to earn.

    Further down the road

    More screens

    • iPhone Duo. A layout that makes the most of the big inner screen, and carries on from the outer one when you fold it shut.
    • Mac. Listen at your desk and manage a large library with a keyboard and pointer.

    Read and listen

    • EPUB read-along. Add the ebook alongside its audiobook, and read along as you listen, with the current sentence highlighted.

    More places for your books

    • Dropbox. Bring in audiobooks from your Dropbox.
    • Google Drive. Bring in audiobooks from your Google Drive.
    • Jellyfin. Connect Novelwave to your Jellyfin server.

    On your Home Screen

    • More widgets. New kinds of widgets, beyond Now Playing.

    Your listening

    • Levels and experience. Earn experience as you listen and level up along the way.
