How to play MP3 audiobooks on iPhone, with chapters

By Mihai Petrenco, who makes Novelwave · Updated November 23, 2026

To play an MP3 audiobook on iPhone and keep your place, import all of the book’s MP3 files together and merge them into one audiobook. In Novelwave, each file then becomes a chapter. A CD rip of 60 tracks or a download in 40 parts then plays as a single book, with one place to come back to and the time left on the whole thing.

Why MP3 audiobooks come in pieces

Audiobook CDs are split into tracks, and many downloads follow the same pattern: one MP3 per chapter or section, often packed into a .zip archive. Libro.fm offers MP3 downloads alongside M4B, and LibriVox offers its public‑domain recordings as MP3 too. Each file on its own is just a track. Merged into one audiobook, they’re a book.

Step 1: Gather the files in one folder

  • From a download: Safari saves it to the Downloads folder in the Files app. If it’s a .zip archive, tap it in Files to unzip it into a folder.
  • From a CD rip on a Mac: AirDrop the tracks to your iPhone and save them to Files, or copy the folder to iCloud Drive.
  • From a Windows PC: copy the folder to iCloud Drive, and it shows up in the Files app on your iPhone.

Before importing, check that every track is there and that the numbering runs in order.

Step 2: Import the files together

  1. In Novelwave (get it on the App Store), open Library, tap + and choose Local Files, or iCloud Drive if that’s where the folder is.
  2. Open the book’s folder and select all of its MP3 files at once.
  3. Tap Save. When Novelwave asks how to save them, choose Merge Into One Audiobook, check the title, author and cover, and save. Each file becomes a chapter.

Picking a whole folder in the iCloud Drive browser does this for you: each folder you pick becomes its own book.

Keeping your audiobooks in iCloud Drive? Go to Profile, then Sources, and connect iCloud Drive first. Novelwave creates its own folder there. Move the book’s folder into it with the Files app, or with iCloud Drive on a Mac or Windows PC, then import from iCloud Drive in Library.

Step 3: Listen, and keep your place

Open the book and press play. The chapters list shows each file, so going back to the start of disc three is one tap away. Novelwave remembers where you are in the book as a whole, shows the time left until the end, and keeps track of the books you’ve finished.

  • A sleep timer that can stop at the end of the chapter, which here means the end of the track.
  • Speed from 0.5× to 6×.
  • Bookmarks with notes that point to a moment in the book, not just a file.
  • Skip intervals for catching a sentence you missed.

Your place, bookmarks and shelves sync with iCloud to your iPad and Apple Watch, so the next chapter can wait for whichever device is closest.

Questions

Do I need to convert my MP3s to M4B first?

No. Novelwave plays the MP3 files as they are. If you already have a book as a single M4B, see how to play M4B audiobooks on iPhone.

My CD rip is in FLAC, WAV or AIFF. Does that work?

Yes. Novelwave plays M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC, Opus, FLAC, WAV and AIFF, as long as the files aren’t DRM‑protected.

Can I take the book to my Apple Watch?

Yes, and listen there with your iPhone left at home. See how to listen to your own audiobooks on Apple Watch.

Can I get my files back out?

Yes. Export a single book or your whole library to Files whenever you like.
