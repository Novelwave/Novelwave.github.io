How to play M4B audiobooks on iPhone

By Mihai Petrenco, who makes Novelwave · Updated November 23, 2026

To play an M4B audiobook on iPhone, save the file to the Files app or iCloud Drive, then import it into an audiobook player that understands M4B chapters. Novelwave reads the chapter markers inside the file, remembers your place, and keeps it in step across your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. From file to first chapter takes a couple of minutes.

What an M4B file is

M4B is the usual format for a DRM‑free audiobook: one file holding the whole book, with chapter markers built in. Libro.fm sells audiobooks you download as M4B or MP3, and LibriVox offers its public‑domain recordings in both formats. If your book came as a folder of MP3 files instead, see how to play MP3 audiobooks on iPhone.

Step 1: Put the M4B file on your iPhone

How you do this depends on where the file is now:

  • On a Mac: AirDrop the file to your iPhone and save it to Files.
  • On a website: download it in Safari on your iPhone. It lands in the Downloads folder in the Files app. If it arrives as a .zip archive, tap it in Files to unzip it.
  • On a Windows PC: copy it to iCloud Drive, and it shows up in the Files app on your iPhone.

Step 2: Import it into Novelwave

  1. Install Novelwave from the App Store and open Library.
  2. Tap + and choose Local Files, or iCloud Drive if that’s where the book is.
  3. Pick the M4B file. You can select as many files as you like at once.

You can also share an M4B from another app, such as Files or a browser download, straight to Novelwave.

To keep a whole collection in iCloud Drive, go to Profile, then Sources, and connect iCloud Drive. Novelwave creates its own folder there. Drop your audiobooks into it with the Files app, or with iCloud Drive on a Mac or Windows PC, then import them from iCloud Drive in Library.

Step 3: Listen, chapter by chapter

Tap the book to start playing. The player’s chapters list comes from the M4B’s own chapter markers, so you can jump straight to part two or back to the prologue. A few things worth setting up:

  • Speed anywhere from 0.5× to 6×.
  • A sleep timer that can stop at the end of the chapter, so you don’t wake up three chapters on.
  • Bookmarks with notes for the lines you want to find again.
  • Skip intervals that suit how you listen.

Your place, bookmarks and shelves sync with iCloud, so you can start a chapter on your iPhone and finish it on your iPad or Apple Watch. Away from the app, the book is on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, in Control Center and in CarPlay.

Questions

Can I play Audible or Apple Books audiobooks this way?

No. Audible’s AAX files and Apple Books purchases are protected with DRM and only play in their own apps. Novelwave plays files you own that aren’t DRM‑protected.

Can’t Apple Books play M4B?

Only once you’ve synced the file from a Mac with Finder: on iPhone, Books can’t import an audiobook from Files or iCloud Drive. Novelwave imports straight from Files and iCloud Drive, merges a book’s MP3 parts into one, plays Opus and FLAC too, and gives the books you own shelves inside shelves and listening stats.

Where can I find M4B audiobooks?

Libro.fm sells DRM‑free audiobooks to download from its website, and LibriVox has public‑domain recordings as MP3 or M4B. Downpour and some publishers’ own stores sell DRM‑free copies too, and you can rip audiobook CDs yourself.

Why doesn’t my M4B show any chapters?

Chapters come from markers inside the file. If the file was made without them, there’s nothing to list, but the book still plays from start to finish and keeps your place.

Can I listen on my Apple Watch?

Yes, even with your iPhone left at home. See how to listen to your own audiobooks on Apple Watch.
